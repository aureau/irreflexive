Trump Urges Congress to Pass Major Healthcare Plan

Healthcare,Donald Trump,US Congress

Posted on AllSides January 16, 2026
Newsmax (News)
From the Right
Newsmax (News)
AllSides Media Bias Rating: Right
See full bias rating for Newsmax (News) Learn about media bias

President Donald Trump on Thursday urged Congress to pass what the White House is calling the "Great Healthcare Plan," a sweeping proposal aimed at lowering healthcare costs, reducing prescription drug prices, and cutting insurance premiums.

At the center of the plan is Trump's long-standing push to lower drug prices by ensuring Americans pay no more than patients in other developed countries.

The proposal would codify Trump's most-favored-nation pricing policy, building on actions taken during his first term to lower insulin costs and on recent executive orders that led to voluntary price-cutting agreements with pharmaceutical companies.
